## Editorial boundary

Public copy must not claim that a visible facade element in a third-party photo was manufactured by Steel Produkt unless such a photo-to-supply link is independently confirmed. Steel Produkt manufactures and supplies products; the portfolio must not state or imply that the company performs installation on site.
